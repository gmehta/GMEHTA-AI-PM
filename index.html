About

AI-native builder,
platform thinking at scale

I'm a Staff Product Manager with 13+ years building platforms and Agentic AI systems — and a builder by default. I don't wait for a brief or a roadmap slot: I start from first principles, identify what's broken or missing, and find a way to build it — often myself, with code, agents, and the tools on my desk.

At Intuit, I lead GTM Technology — owning the end-to-end marketing and customer engagement platform stack spanning MarTech (AEP, Marketo, Eloqua), SalesTech, and AdTech — while pioneering an industry-first agentic campaign operations system presented at the CTO Deep Dive. The flagship projects and agent demos on this site aren't slide decks; they're products I designed, built, and shipped.

Before Intuit, I spent 8 years at Fanatics building the CRM, personalization, and omnichannel commerce platform that powered $350M+ in annual revenue across 18 billion messages per year — including the LightHouse recommendation engine that alone contributed $125M.

My focus: architect platforms where MarTech, SalesTech, and AdTech converge — and layer Agentic AI on top so teams can move faster, with less overhead, and deliver experiences at a scale that previously wasn't possible.
